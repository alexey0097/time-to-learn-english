Big Update: 33 New Clubs on the Site!

September 24, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Friends, the site has just had its biggest update ever! 🎉 Our club catalog has grown from 9 to 42 clubs — speaking clubs, game nights, literature circles, stand-up comedy, language exchanges and even football. Each new club has its own page with photos from real meetings, a schedule, venues, prices and how to sign up.

What's new

  • 🗣 More speaking clubs for every level and every day of the week — from cozy Sunday meetups like Chatty Chillies and Just Talk to thought-provoking debates at English Chatter Club.
  • 🎲 Board games and Mafia in English — Goose Club, English Games Club and V&K English Speaking Club.
  • 📚 Literature and film clubs — read a short story a week at English Literature Club or Long Story Short, or discuss movies at Moscow English Conversation Club.
  • 🎤 Stand-up comedy in English — Stand Up Club in English and Clean Open Mic.
  • 🌍 Language exchanges and international parties — MoscowLEM, ISN HSE Moscow, Club de Español en Moscú and International VIP.
  • 💻 Online clubs — join from anywhere: English Starts Today!, LinguaClub and Girl's Speaking Club.
  • ⚽ And something different — play football with locals and expats at Moscow International Football.

Find your club in a minute

Open the Clubs page and use the filters: choose the type of meeting (speed meetings, board games, group meetings or online), free or paid, and see who meets today or this week. Click any card to read the full story of the club.

Know a club that's still missing? Write to us on @eng_events — we'd be happy to add it. See you at the meetings! ✨

New Here?

Most clubs are very beginner-friendly. Just come with a positive attitude and a willingness to chat. Here is what to expect at different types of events:

Speaking Clubs
Casual conversation in small groups or one-on-one. Topics are usually provided. No pressure, just talk.
Online Clubs
Join from anywhere via Zoom or Telegram. Perfect if you are shy or short on time.
Game Nights
Practice English while playing board games or Mafia. A relaxed way to learn.
Stand-Up & Open Mics
Enjoy comedy in English. Some events are free, others require a ticket.

Have a great week and happy speaking!
