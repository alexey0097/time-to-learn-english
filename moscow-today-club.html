Moscow Today Club

September 27, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Moscow Today has been getting people together to speak English since August 2023 — first as Saturday board game nights, now as a speaking club that meets twice a week. It is run by Dima, an American living in Moscow, so the room is in English from the first minute, and other native speakers from the community often come along too.

The idea is simple, and the club repeats it in every announcement: on a course you listen for 90% of the time and speak for minutes. Here you speak from the moment you sit down. You get a new conversation partner every 15 minutes — no teacher, no textbook, no homework, and nobody correcting every word. Say "he go" instead of "he goes" and the table will laugh with you and carry on.

Turning up is the whole procedure: come to Surf Coffee on Ostozhenka, order your drink at the counter, say you're with the club, and someone will sit you down next to a friendly person who was also nervous once. Just come a little early — there aren't many tables and they fill up fast. 🙌

Our Format

Format: a rotating speaking club in a café. You talk in pairs and small groups, and swap partners every 15 minutes, so you never get stuck in one conversation and everyone gets the same amount of speaking time.

  • 🗣️ A new partner every 15 minutes — an hourglass on the table keeps everyone honest
  • ☕ No teacher, no textbook, no homework — just conversation from the first minute
  • 🇺🇸 Native speakers in the room — the host is American, and other native speakers come along too
  • 🤍 Any level is fine — when there are enough people the groups get split roughly into A2 and B2+
  • 🎲 Games turn up too — a card or word game often lands on the table alongside the talking
  • ⏰ Come early — the café has a limited number of tables and they go quickly

Ordinary club evenings from the end of August to the end of September 2026 — most of them finish with everyone squeezing in for one photo.

Special Formats

🎲 Board game nights

The club's original format, and the one the regulars still talk about: a long table, a stack of games and four hours of English until the café asks everyone to leave. 🥹 Codenames, Bunker, Harry Potter Monopoly, Cards Against Humanity, Taboo, Alias, Spyfall, Who Am I?, Imaginarium and Scrabble all make the rounds. Through the winter these ran on Saturday evenings at Karavaev Brothers (Sushchevskaya 25s1, m. Mendeleevskaya, exit 4) — 500 ₽ cash or transfer, with 30% off the food through the café's own app. In the warm months they moved outdoors to Gorky Park, at Surf Coffee on the Pushkinskaya Embankment, with badminton and table tennis alongside the games. Watch the chat for the next one.

Saturday game nights — the long table at Karavaev Brothers through autumn and winter, and the first tables outdoors in May.

Registration

There is no ticket and no booking — just show up. If you're planning to come, the host asks you to write a "+" in the chat so he knows how many chairs to keep. 👋

  • Club channel: @moscowtodayhq

The same chat is also where Moscow Today posts free things to do around the city — festivals, free museum days, new parks — so it's a decent follow even on the weeks you can't make it.
