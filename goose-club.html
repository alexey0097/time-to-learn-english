Board games - Goose Club

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

The Polyglot IGROTEKA! 🪿 Igra Ütka (Goose) Club is a board game club for language lovers, run by a Belgian host at Geektime anticafe near Mendeleevskaya. Every Tuesday is English night (plus other languages), and every Wednesday is French night.

Great modern board games, tea and cookies, an international crowd — and a lot of speaking without even noticing it. 🎲

Our Format

⏱ A classic evening:

  • 19:00 — doors open: chatting, 20-minute warm-up games, tea, cookies and food (bring your own drinks, no alcohol)
  • 19:45 — everyone chooses which big game to play — 1, 2 or 3 tables
  • 20:00–22:00 — the big game
  • 23:00 — official end (the most passionate play until the metro closes 😄)

🎲 Games: Forest Shuffle, 7 Wonders, Cellulose, Cerberus, Château Combo, Flip Seven, Smallworld — and guests often bring their own.

🗣️ Language: Tuesday — English + other languages; Wednesday — French + other languages, unless everyone at a table agrees on one language.

Special Formats

🚆 Club Trips

Sometimes the club travels together — for example, a French-speaking trip to Voronezh with walks, museums and, of course, board games.

Registration

No registration — just come to Geektime on Tuesday evening. Payment follows the anticafe's rules.
