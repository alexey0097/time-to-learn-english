Girl's Speaking Club

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

"I understand a lot in English, but when I need to speak... I just can't say a word." 💗 Sound familiar? Girls' Speaking Club is an online English club for women who want to travel, move abroad, make friends from all over the world or confidently go on dates with foreigners.

The idea is simple: more grammar won't remove the fear of speaking. You don't need to be perfect to start speaking — you need to start speaking to become confident. Come even with your imperfect English. ✨

Our Format

Format: a Sunday speaking club in Zoom in a cozy, "girly" atmosphere. Every month has its own theme, with movie clubs and game nights along the way.

📅 Autumn 2026 program:

  • September — The Modern Woman: "Beautiful or Smart?", feminism and gender roles, Movie Club: Barbie, "Girl Code: Competition or Sisterhood?"
  • October — Body & Wellness: food and feelings, cognitive biases, atomic habits, a wellness game night
  • November — Treat Yourself: self-care, autumn rituals and hygge, a movie club on Bridget Jones's Diary

📚 Vocabulary: after each month you get flashcards with the top words and expressions — FOMO vs JOMO, Sex and the City phrases, what makes a good friend, social confidence.

📸 From our meetings

Most meetings happen in Zoom, but in July 2026 the host met club members offline in Moscow — tea and English conversation around one table.

Before a movie club you watch the film in English with subtitles, then discuss it: Eat, Pray, Love, Sex and the City, Barbie.
