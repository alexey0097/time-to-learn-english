English Literature Club

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

One short story a week — and a whole evening to talk about it. 📚 English Literature Club meets at Svetlovka, the Central City Youth Library, to discuss short fiction in English: from classics like Kurt Vonnegut, Graham Greene and Katherine Mansfield to contemporary voices like Ted Chiang, China Miéville and Patricia Lockwood.

The club also hosts discussion evenings on films and big questions — from "Captain Fantastic" to whether artificial intelligence will kill our interest in science and creativity.

Our Format

Story Discussion: every week the club picks one short story and posts it in the channel together with an introduction to the author and often an audio version. Read the story beforehand — then come and share your impressions. The language of the meeting is English.

📖 Recent stories:

  • "The Destructors" by Graham Greene
  • "Details" by China Miéville
  • "The School" by Donald Barthelme
  • "The Foster Portfolio" by Kurt Vonnegut
  • "Hell Is the Absence of God" by Ted Chiang

💬 Discussion evenings: on some Fridays the club discusses a film or a topic — dreams, censorship and freedom of speech, the books we read, AI and creativity. The film link or background text is posted in advance.
