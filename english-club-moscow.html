ENGLISH CLUB 🛩️☁️ MOSCOW

September 27, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

We sign everything with a little plane and a cloud 🛩️☁️, and that is pretty much the whole idea — "the world gets bigger when you understand English". The club grew out of the 128bpm studio community in the summer of 2025 and it has stayed that way ever since: English mixed with the things we'd be doing anyway — films, series, music, coffee and stretching.

Our host is Chi Chi (Galina Chiblis): eight years living in the States, a degree from Moscow State Linguistic University, eleven years of teaching and a well-known "slang English" series. The club was started together with Miss Helen, who ran the pronunciation and accent sessions.

It isn't a lesson. It's an hour — on a video call, or in a room in the centre of Moscow — where you talk about the film everyone has just watched, laugh, get things wrong and leave with a page of new words. The crowd is mostly girls, and some events are announced as girls-only, but the café meet-ups are for everyone: "boys and girls waiting for youuuuu".

Our Format

Format: a one-hour English club — on a video call, and every few weeks offline in central Moscow. A warm-up quiz, a discussion of the topic, speaking practice and mini-tasks, then a vocabulary list you take home with you.

  • 🛩️ A comfortable middle level — not aimed at advanced speakers, and no overload
  • ☁️ Live talk, not a textbook — everything starts from a film, a series, a book or a song in the original
  • 📚 Homework you'll actually do — Quizlet flashcards with the words from the session, and an episode to re-watch
  • 🎤 Guest native speakers from the US and Canada drop into the calls

💬 What we've talked about: the film The Housemaid, Desperate Housewives, Euphoria, Britney Spears' memoir The Woman in Me, Megan Fox's poems Pretty Boys Are Poisonous, High School Musical — plus line-by-line breakdowns of songs by Britney Spears, will.i.am and Travis Scott.

Special Formats

🎥 Movie nights

Sometimes we book a block of seats at the cinema and watch a film in English with Russian subtitles — Look At Me: XXXTentacion was one of them. Sometimes we stay in instead: the Halloween night came with spooky treats, tea, a film we'd voted for and a discussion afterwards.

🧘 Coffee rave & stretching

A Saturday that starts at 9:30 in a dance school: English practice with Miss Helen and Chi Chi, a stretch class, a barista making coffee and a DJ set by Chi Chi. In the club's own words — "begin your day with 3 hours invested in yourself".

🍿 Cinema Stretch

At the offline meetings the English hour is followed by an hour of gentle stretching while the film plays. Two hours, two formats — a bit of English, a bit of movement and a lot of talking.

🎧 Trap yoga in English

Where the whole thing started, at the 128bpm yoga festival: Chi Chi played a DJ set, Miss Helen led the shavasana meditation in English, and the English hour came right after. We handed out the vocabulary first — inhale, exhale, sinking down into the mat.

Price

Café meet-ups: free. Places are limited — the first one had 20 seats — so put your name down in the chat as soon as it's announced.

The Saturday online hour: announced in the chat with a joining link; no fee has ever been mentioned for it.

Ticketed events: the bigger ones are sold through Timepad and the 128bpm app. The Halloween movie night was 3,500 ₽, with 15% off the total if you bought two tickets at once.

Courses: Chi Chi also runs a separate closed online course — modern spoken English, slang and song breakdowns — at 8,888 ₽ for eight lessons a month.

The chat is private, so the invite link is the way in — and once you're there you get everything: the joining link for the online hour, the meet-up announcements and the sign-up lists.
