Bright School N1

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

English Club with native speakers. 🎩 Bright School N1 is a language school on Izmailovsky Boulevard that regularly opens its doors for English speaking clubs with guests from abroad. Over the years the club has been hosted by Danny and Michael from England, Keith from New Zealand, Kathie from the USA, Ali from Oxford — and now by Dale from Oxford. It is a relaxed conversation in English with a real native speaker.

Our Format

English Club: every meeting has its own topic chosen by the host — "What Britain Is Really Like", "Cockney as a Dialect of Londoners", "Good Habits — Bad Habits", "Everyday Heroes", "Global Citizens and the Most In-Demand Professions". Sometimes there is a team quiz or even a smorgasbord. Photo reports from the meetings appear in the channel and on the school's website.

🎬 Movie courses: in summer the school runs film sessions for adults — for example, reading the script of "Roman Holiday" with Audrey Hepburn and Gregory Peck by roles, analyzing, watching and discussing it.

📚 Also at the school: an adult conversational English group, a foreign literature course (from the Brothers Grimm to Dickens, Shakespeare and Oscar Wilde) and language courses — English, Russian for foreigners and now Chinese with a native speaker.
