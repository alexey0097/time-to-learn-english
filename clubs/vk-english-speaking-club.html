V&K English Speaking Club

Think you can spot the imposter? Or can you spin a lie so convincing that everyone believes you? 😈 V&K English Speaking Club gathers every Saturday at a cozy coffee shop on Arbat for Mafia in English, party games and live conversation.

Whether you're a seasoned player or a total newbie, it's your chance to test your wits and have a blast with great people! 🎉

Our Format

Format: a 2-hour game evening in English.

  • 🕵️ Mafia — a night of strategy, bluffs and suspense. The Dons are getting more cunning, but the town keeps pace!
  • 🎲 "Bet you can" and "Emotions" — party games with hot topics to discuss
  • ☕ Simple live communication over great coffee

📚 Grammar box: between meetings the channel posts short grammar reminders — handy for revising the basics.
