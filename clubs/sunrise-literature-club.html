Sunrise Literature Club

Welcome to SUNRISE Literature Discussion Club! 📚 Every Wednesday evening, readers gather at a Moscow library to discuss short stories, plays and poetry in English — from classics like H. G. Wells and F. S. Fitzgerald to contemporary writers.

This is a club for those who love a good text and a deep conversation. The club even keeps up correspondence with living authors: members send their questions to the writers — T. C. Boyle and Geoffrey Becker among them — and share the answers with everyone in the channel.

Our Format

Format: a literature discussion. Each month has its own theme, and each week the club discusses one piece. The text is posted in the channel about a week before the meeting, so you have time to read it.

📖 Monthly themes, for example:

  • July — ending with the play "Mrs. Warren's Profession" by George Bernard Shaw
  • August — discovering India: stories by Ruskin Bond, Jhumpa Lahiri and Anita Desai, and the poetry of Rabindranath Tagore
  • September — "the phantom music of traditions": Roald Dahl, William Golding, H. G. Wells, F. S. Fitzgerald and an essay by George Saunders on Turgenev's "The Singers"

🎵 Between meetings the channel shares poems, paintings and music that echo the stories under discussion.
