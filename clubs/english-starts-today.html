English Starts Today!

Start your weekend in English! ⚡️ "Сегодня Английский!" (English Starts Today!) is an open free online speaking club in Zoom that has been bringing people together for the third year. Every Saturday dozens of participants — from Moscow and from all over the globe — join to talk about questions that really matter.

Human connection is one of the main values in life — and that's exactly why the club exists: to meet interesting people, learn something new, exchange opinions and look at familiar things from different angles. 🔥

Our Format

Format: live conversation in pairs and mini-groups around the topic of the week — "Public opinion", "Popular culture", "What makes us truly happy" and more.

⏱ 90 minutes in Zoom, 12:00–13:30 Moscow time.

🔸 Level: A2–B2.

📒 Workbook: before the meeting, open the online workbook — it's updated live during the session.

🌍 International friends often join too — a great chance to talk with people from different countries.

Special Formats

🏃 Speaking Marathon

In summer the club ran a six-week speaking marathon — an online meeting every evening from 19:00 to 20:00, each night on a new topic.

🌳 Offline Meetups in Moscow

Sometimes the online community meets in real life — for example, for a summer walk and picnic in a Moscow park.

Price

Saturday club: free.

Registration

The announcement and registration link for each Saturday meeting are published in the channel on Monday at 10:00 — places fill up fast (sometimes 100+ applications in a few days!). The Zoom link arrives by email two hours before the start.
