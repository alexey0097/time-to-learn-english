English at IRISHMAN

English in an Irish pub — what could be more natural? 🍀 English at IRISHMAN is a speaking club that meets in the cozy halls of the IRISHMAN pubs. Two meetings a week in two parts of the city, and one of them is led by a native speaker.

A warm company around a big wooden table, a pint of something tasty and live conversation in English — everyone is welcome!

Our Format

Format: a speaking club with a coach who guides the conversation.

  • Wednesday — the club with a native speaker, Coach Steve. Recommended level: B2+
  • 🍀 Sunday — the club with Coach Anna. Recommended level: B1+

👥 A meeting takes place when at least 4 participants sign up — so feel free to invite your friends and colleagues!
