Ziferblat English Speaking Club

Tucked away in a beautiful historic buildings, our speaking club is nothing like a classroom. There are no desks, no whiteboards, and no pressure to get your grammar perfect. Instead, you'll find soft armchairs, warm lamplight, a piano in the corner, and the smell of fresh coffee — the unmistakable atmosphere of Ziferblat.

We are part of the world's first pay-per-minute cultural space, a social experiment that reimagines what a public place can be. Here, you pay only for the time you spend. Everything else — tea, coffee, cookies, WiFi, board games, books, and that piano — is included. It's a place designed for work, creativity, and connection. And once a week, it transforms into a vibrant English-speaking living room.

Our club is built on a simple belief: language lives in conversation, not in textbooks. Whether you are an expat navigating life in Moscow or a learner who understands a lot but still freezes when it's your turn to speak, you are genuinely welcome here.

Our Format

Every session is facilitated by a warm, experienced host whose job is to make sure the conversation never stalls and nobody feels left out. Think of them less as a teacher and more as the friend who always knows how to start a good discussion.

A typical evening unfolds like this:

  • ✨ Warm-up & icebreakers. A quick game or a light question to switch your brain into English mode. Simple, fun, never awkward.
  • 🔄 Small rotating groups. We split up and shuffle throughout the session so you speak with different people, hear different accents, and keep the energy fresh.
  • 💬 Guided but free-flowing topics. The host offers prompts — travel memories, cultural quirks, the joy and chaos of life in Moscow — but the conversation always finds its own path.
  • 🎲 Games & improvisation. Sometimes we bring out board games, storytelling cards, or improv exercises. It's astonishing how fast you forget you're speaking a foreign language when you're busy trying to win a game.
  • ☕ Coffee in hand, all evening long. Refill your mug as many times as you like. Grab cookies. Get comfortable.

Mistakes are celebrated here. They're proof you're trying. Nobody corrects you mid-sentence. Nobody grades you. The only goal is to connect, express yourself, and enjoy the process.

The Team

At the heart of the club is our host and community builder. When you arrive, they greet you at the door like an old friend, explain how the space works, and help you settle in before the session begins.

Our hosts are fluent English speakers and skilled facilitators. They know how to gently invite the quietest person into the conversation without pressure, and how to let a lively discussion breathe without losing structure. They come from diverse backgrounds — some are expats who found a home at Ziferblat themselves, others are Moscow locals who've spent years abroad — but they all share a passion for bringing people together through language.

Behind the scenes, the wider Ziferblat team keeps the space running with the same care you'd put into your own living room. The result is a place that somehow feels both vibrant and deeply calm at the same time.
