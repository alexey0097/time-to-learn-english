Dark English Academia

Moscow-based and globally minded. 🖤 Dark English Academia (D'CLUB) is a community for "global minds" that mixes online discussion clubs with English trips around Moscow — galleries, parks, festivals and even embassies. The host is an international student living in Moscow, so English here is the natural common language.

Level or age doesn't matter — "we're not English police or followers of ageism". What matters is curiosity and the wish to talk about ideas. 🌍

Our Format

The club runs several types of meetings, each marked with its level:

  • 🔴 C1+ Speaking Club — advanced discussions like "Traveling vs Living Abroad" or "Expat vs Immigrant"
  • 🔴 B1+ Speaking Club — for example, "Emotive Ambience": how to describe Russian beauty culture in English without sliding into "it's nice"
  • 🔴 TC+ Thinking Club — not just talking but reading: literature in advance, a 10-minute presentation, then a discussion based on what you've read
  • 🔴 English Trips & Visits — the Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow Zoo, Sergiev Posad, a coffee and tea festival — practicing descriptive vocabulary on the go
  • 🔴 Play "Mafia" — the classic game, English only, trust no one 💀
