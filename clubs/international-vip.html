Group Meetings

International VIP

One rule: mix or go home. 😈 Moscow International Events (MIE) throws the most international parties in the city: 150+ people, 20+ nationalities and zero awkward standing around. You meet new friends, socialize, practice languages — and then you party!

Every party has its own theme — Ferragosto, Ibiza Vibes, Egyptian White, Barcelona Night or "Welcome Back (to the Future)" with four decades of hits. It's one of the easiest ways to speak English with people from all over the world.

Our Format

Format: a themed international party, usually once a month on Friday from 19:00.

  • 🎟 MIE wristband + Icebreaker game — perfect for solo "travelers" and shy people
  • 🍹 Welcome drink for every guest
  • 🎧 International DJs with the best hits
  • 👗 A light dress code for the theme — for example, one item in white

💬 Language: English is the common language of the evening — and you'll hear Spanish, Italian, Arabic and many others around you.

Where we meet

Day Venue Address
Friday, 19:00 (monthly) Soho Rooms Savvinskaya nab., 12/8

At the door say the password INTERNATIONAL. 🔑 The date and theme of the next party are announced in the channel.

Special Formats

🚀 Community Events

Between the big parties, MIE members organize smaller gatherings — informal Friday meetups, English stand-up nights with friends from English Stand-up in Moscow, and even a free science lecture in a bar: "Cocktails & Astrophysics" by an aerospace engineer from the community.

Price

International Party: 500 ₽ from 19:00 to 20:00, 1000 ₽ after — a welcome drink is included. Cash at the door.
