English Games Club

Communicative board games in English. 🎲 English Games Club is a free community for beginners (A1–A2 and up) who want to break the language barrier through games. Speaking clubs feel too intense? Here you practise English while playing simple, fun board and party games — "the calmest way to practise English".

The club has only one rule: if you come, you speak with everyone else in English.

Our Format

⏱ A typical evening (19:00–21:00):

  • a short introduction and a 15-minute warm-up in English about yourself, your week or the holidays
  • fun ice-breaker games to get to know each other
  • 2–3 simple board games — no stress, only laughter

🎲 Games: Codenames (bought with donations from members), Taboo, Apples to Apples, Snake Oil, Spyfall, the Hat game, Snakes and Ladders with questions and dilemmas, making up stories from picture cards. For bigger groups (6–8+ players) there are Sounds Like a Plan and Bunker.

🗣️ Level: announced for each meeting — usually A1+ or A2+. Hosts adapt the rules to the players' level.

Special Formats

🌳 Games in the park

In summer the club sometimes moves outdoors to Muzeon park: a warm-up talk and 2–3 games sitting on the grass. Bring a mat or a blanket, water and mosquito repellent. The meeting takes place only if the weather is good.

📚 Learning meetings

From time to time the hosts run learning meetings for beginners: grammar practice (e.g. Present Simple and Past Simple) and building sentences with words from the Taboo cards, followed by a game. Payment is a donation of any amount.

💻 Online games

Occasionally the club tests online versions of games such as Taboo and Story Dice.

Price

The game nights are free — you only pay for your own order at the cafe. Donations are welcome and go towards buying new games for the club. 🎁

Registration

Meetings are announced in the chat. If you are coming, put a 👍 under the announcement — a meeting takes place when at least three people are ready to come.
