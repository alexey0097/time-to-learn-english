ISN HSE Moscow

Come for the language practice and stay for the people. 👐 ISN HSE — the International Student Network of the Higher School of Economics — brings together international and local students in Moscow. Its most beloved tradition is the Tandem Meeting: an evening of chatting, connecting and practicing languages in a fun, laid-back atmosphere.

And the best part — Tandem meetings are open to everyone, not only HSE students. Whether you're a Tandem pro or a first-timer, even if you're coming solo, the vibe is always friendly and inclusive. ✨

Our Format

Format: a language exchange in a bar. International and Russian speakers mix at the tables and switch between English, Russian and other languages — you practice the language you're learning and help others with yours.

👥 Who comes: international students, local students and anyone who wants to meet people from different countries. The event is 18+, so bring your passport.

🎟 Places: limited to about 50 people, so register in advance.

Special Formats

🧺 Picnic&Talk

In summer, Tandem moves outdoors. Together with AEGEE, ISN hosts language picnics in Tsaritsyno Park on weekend afternoons: bring a blanket and some snacks, play board games and have easy-going conversations in the open air.

🚆 One-Day Trips

Guided trips to historic towns around Moscow — Kolomna, Serpukhov, Zvenigorod, Istra — with a walking tour, local places and a great company to speak English with.

🌍 Culture Café

Evenings at HSE where international students present their home countries — food, traditions and stories from Sudan, Indonesia, Pakistan, Syria and more. (Culture Café events are usually for HSE students only.)

📸 From our trips and outings
