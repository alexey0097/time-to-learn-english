CherryPie Club

CherryPie Club is a language community in the very heart of Moscow, just 2 minutes from Tverskaya metro station. Several evenings a week people gather here to talk in English, play board games, sing karaoke and argue over deduction games — all in a warm, relaxed atmosphere with tea, snacks and homemade chocolate muffins. 🍒

Whatever your mood — a calm chat, a noisy game night or a movie — there is a format for every day of the week.

Our Format

Format: speed meetings — two hours of speaking, asking questions, debating and exchanging opinions and impressions with different people in pairs and small groups. After the talking part, it's time for board games.

⏱ Weekly schedule:

  • Tuesday, 19:30 — English Meeting + Board Games: speaking in pairs and small groups, then brain-challenging speaking games like Codenames, Snake Oil and Alias
  • Friday, 19:30 — Friday English Meeting + Karaoke or Board Games (optional)
  • Saturday, 17:30 — The Resistance: Avalon
  • Sunday — Teen's Club online and Movie Club (see below)

🍪 Sweet bonus: tea and snacks are included, and on Tuesdays the hosts bake chocolate muffins for everyone.

Special Formats

Besides the regular speaking evenings, CherryPie hosts formats where English is the language of the game — you practice without even noticing.

👑 The Resistance: Avalon

A game where players attempt to deduce one another's identities. It's a lot like Mafia, but nobody gets killed — just debates, mischief-making, well-staged strategies and total distrust of your table companions. Lots of discussion, everyone is so suspicious... and you will love it! Tea and snacks are included. Registration is required.

💥 The enQUIZition — English Pub Quiz

An intellectual pub quiz in English, usually on Sundays at 16:00 at cafe "Gauguin" (Nizhnyaya Syromyatnicheskaya ul., 10, m. Kurskaya). Register a team of 2 to 8 people — or come alone and the hosts will find you a team on the spot. The top three teams get prizes. 🏆

🎬 English Movie Club

Sunday afternoons at "Sofit" dedicated to Oscar-winning movies in English. Tea, snacks and popcorn are included — just book your seat in advance.

📚 Teen's Club

An online speaking club for teenagers aged 14–16 on Sundays at 11:00. The main idea is simple — to make teens talk: interesting topics, board games and lots of fun.

Club de Español

Learning Spanish too? On Thursdays at 19:30 the club hosts a Spanish conversation meeting with native speakers at Stars Coffee (ul. Arbat, 39, m. Smolenskaya), levels A2–C2.

Registration

Choose the format and sign up on the club's website — for Avalon, the quiz and Movie Club registration is required.
