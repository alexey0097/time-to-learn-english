Nastushka's Speaking Club

"Four years ago, I simply created a place where people could speak English." 💌 That's how Nastushka describes her club — and over these years it grew into a big, warm community: thousands of conversations, friendships, journeys, stories, laughs, arguments, ideas, games and even love.

Every Friday evening a full room gathers at the library to practice English and have fun. People who might have never met otherwise end up sitting in the same circle — and often become friends. In September 2026 the club celebrated its 4th birthday! 🎉

Our Format

Format: an informal speaking club with a new topic every Friday — siblings, honesty, reality and the mind, hygge and much more. Expect random conversations, good vibes and sometimes a little "homework" in the channel. ☕

🎤 Guests and interviews: interesting people from the community come to share their stories — for example, an actor from Liberty Theater, a musical theater in English.

👥 Atmosphere: friendly, a bit chaotic in the best way, and always open-hearted.

Special Formats

🎉 Birthday Party

Every September the club throws a birthday party: party hats, costumes, balloons, food to share (if you can cook — cook!), toasts and best wishes. The room gets so full that Nastushka jokes they'll need Luzhniki next year. 😄

Price

Friday meeting: free. The club runs on the organizer's energy, so donations are warmly welcome — details are in the channel.

Registration

Register in the comments under the weekly announcement post — it helps to know how many people are coming.
