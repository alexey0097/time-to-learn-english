Club de Español en Moscú

Spanglish & Ruñol! 💬 Club de Español en Moscú is a big international community of Spanish, Latin American and Russian speakers — and a great place to practice English too. Every Friday they meet in the city center for a language exchange that smoothly turns into an after-work party.

International atmosphere, new friends, useful professional contacts… and maybe something more. 😉

Our Format

Format: Language Exchange + After Work + Networking + Party. People meet at the bar counter, without tables — a more dynamic and social format that makes it easy to get to know everyone.

⏱ Friday evening:

  • 19:00 — language exchange: Spanish · English · Russian
  • 21:00 — immersive shows, dancing, games, live music, DJ and party 🍹

🥃 A welcome shot for the first guests.

Where we meet

Day Venue
Friday, 19:00 Bar "Santa Muerte" (city center) — map

Special Formats

📺 Series in Spanish

Watch the Spanish comedy "El vecino" together, then play games and activities after each episode, with Spanish and Latin American food around. Any level of Spanish is welcome — come alone or with friends. Thursdays at 20:00 at Mamá Juana (1-ya Brestskaya, 10s4, m. Mayakovskaya).

💃 Latin Parties

The community also throws big Latin parties in Moscow clubs — reggaeton, salsa and bachata master classes, live shows and DJs from all over Latin America. A night out with people from all over the world.

Price

Friday Language Exchange: free with registration.

Series in Spanish: 1000 ₽ per session (700 ₽ for students and foreigners); a 5-session pass — 800 ₽ / 500 ₽ per session. Food is paid separately.

Parties: usually 1000 ₽, 500 ₽ for women and students when bought online in advance.
