Just Talk English Speaking Club

Let's chill and share our time with our amazing English community! ❤️ Just Talk is a friendly speaking club that meets twice a week: cozy conversations on Thursdays and board game afternoons on Saturdays.

Big round tables, unlimited tea and cookies, and a warm community of regulars who are always happy to see new faces. ☺️

Our Format

Thursday meetup: a conversation club around one topic of the week — travelling, cooking, the future, pets, science, beauty, internet anonymity and more.

Saturday games: English board games, from party classics to strategy games:

  • 🙊 Taboo
  • 🍏 Apples to Apples
  • 🕵️ Codenames
  • 👑 Citadels — and sometimes something new, like Kingdomino
