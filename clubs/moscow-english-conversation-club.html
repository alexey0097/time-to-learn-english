Moscow English Conversation Club

If you want to use your English, it's best to have something good and fun to talk about. 🎬 Moscow English Conversation Club (MECC) meets every Friday evening to discuss films — classics like "Rear Window" and "A Fish Called Wanda", modern hits like "The Holdovers" and everything in between.

The club was built around the idea of its co-founder Neil, a native speaker from Chicago who spent 25 years helping people improve their conversational English through movies and TV. Today the club keeps the tradition going — and Neil still joins the discussions online from time to time. 🎥

Our Format

Format: a film discussion club. Members vote for a movie in the channel, watch it at home during the week, and on Friday gather around a big table to talk about it.

💬 Questions you might discuss: Can you hear the accents — who's British and who's American? Why do characters act the way they do? What would you have done?

📚 Between meetings the channel shares idioms, articles and film reviews to help you prepare.

🎞 Recent films: "The Holdovers", "Babygirl", "Cold Souls", "Zero Effect", "Rear Window", "A Love Song for Bobby Long", "The Roses".

Special Formats

🕵️ Mafia in English

During the summer break the club sometimes hosts a two-hour Mafia game in English at its usual place.

🎄 Christmas Party

The last meeting of the year is a party with a festive movie ("It's a Wonderful Life"), contests and surprises.
