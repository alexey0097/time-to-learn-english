Social English by Leonid

Free (and now also low-cost) English speaking practice, in pairs and small groups. Organized by Leonid Kokovin & Andrey Gorbatov.

Every Monday, 19:00, a small group gathers at Surf Coffee® x Ostozhenka (Ostozhenka St., 40/1, near Park Kultury) for free English conversation practice — no registration, no fees, minimum level B1. As the group has grown, we’ve added a second weekly meeting: every Thursday, 19:30, a paid session (500 ₽) at the same place.

We also run free English-speaking walks around Moscow — Sparrow Hills, MSU, wherever the route takes us that week — same «just show up» spirit, still completely free.

Our Format

We rotate between pairs and small circles of 3–4 so everyone actually gets to talk (not just listen to the one confident extrovert). Some weeks it’s straight conversation practice; some weeks it’s board games; some weeks it’s a walking tour through Moscow, practicing English the whole way.

Who Shows Up

Regulars, first-timers, people prepping for a move abroad, people who just miss speaking English out loud. We ask for B1 or above so conversations move at a good pace — ages and backgrounds are otherwise mixed on purpose.
