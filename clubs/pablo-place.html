Pablo Place - The Art of Gatherings

No lessons. Just English in real life. 🍀 Pablo Place is an English community at MEGA Khimki for adults, teens and kids. The idea is the same for everyone: don't just study the language — start using the English you already have. You can learn English for years, or you can simply meet up and start talking.

The host, Pablo, is a language coach. Adults meet on Saturday evenings and Tuesday mornings, and kids and teens have their own playful groups — offline in a cozy glass space in the middle of the food court and online in Zoom.

For Adults

Format: live English meetings — offline on Saturdays and online on Tuesday mornings. Instead of the usual "what's your favourite food?", the host likes to bring up real questions: what's on your mind lately, what would you ask a wise person if you could get one honest answer?

👥 Level: any. Beginners who are just starting to speak and confident speakers meet in the same circle — and that's totally fine.

💬 The idea: English here is a way to share thoughts and meet people, not another grammar exercise. Someone will express an idea in two words, someone will tell a whole story.

For Kids and Teens

Kids' groups at Pablo Place are about English through play. From the very first minutes children are immersed in an English-speaking environment: they move, play, laugh — and step by step get used to using English rather than just memorizing words. No complicated tasks: choose cat or dog, answer in one word, show something, ask again, laugh together — and that's already real interaction in English. 😊

📚 Topics change every few weeks — "Animals" (with a trip to a pet shop to watch parrots and fish), "People in my life: friends & family", "Activity" outdoors and more.

👦 Groups: 5–6 years, 7+, PRO 8+ (for kids who already speak and want more practice), 10+ and teens 14–17.

🪙 Pablo Coins

Kids earn their own currency for taking part and practicing on their own, and exchange it for club merch and prizes:

  • +1 for every offline or online meeting, +1 bonus if a child especially stood out
  • +5 for completed home practice
  • Spend them on a bracelet (10), a Pablo Place keychain (15), a Pablo Stick (20) or a photo frame (35) — or save them up. The child decides! 😊
