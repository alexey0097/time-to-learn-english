What is Viva Lingua Club?

Viva Lingua Club is a cosy, discussion-based speaking club in Moscow that has been bringing people together through live conversation since 2009. We've run over 5,000 meetups and built a warm community of learners who believe that the best way to master a language is to speak it — with real people, about real things. We offer regular meetups in English and French, hosted by native speakers in small, welcoming groups.

No textbooks. No lectures. Just lively conversations, new friends, and the kind of practice that actually makes you more fluent. 🗣️✨

What we offer

🎙️ English Speaking Club — discussion meetups on fresh, thoughtfully chosen topics. The host brings carefully selected vocabulary and questions to help you express yourself naturally. For Pre-Intermediate and above.

🎬 KinoClub — watch a film or a short episode, then discuss it together in English. You train your listening skills, pick up real-life phrases, and share your take on the story. For Pre-Intermediate and above.

French Speaking Club — discussions en français with a native speaker for B1–C1 levels. Same cosy format, same focus on lively conversation and useful vocabulary.

🧑‍🏫 One-on-one sessions — personalised lessons with a native speaker, tailored to your goals, level, and interests. English or French, any level.

How every meetup works

We keep things simple so you can focus on speaking:

  • Tiny groups — maximum 7 people. Everyone speaks, no one hides in the corner.
  • Native speaker host — guides the conversation, keeps it flowing, and gives gentle support exactly where you need it.
  • Curated materials — every topic comes with a set of questions and key vocabulary, prepared in advance so you leave with something new.
  • Warm, laid-back atmosphere — we sit around with tea, talk, laugh, and help each other. Zero judgment, plenty of encouragement.
  • In-person, central Moscow — because real-life connection beats a screen.

Why choose Viva Lingua Club

⚡ You actually start speaking. Many learners understand a lot but freeze when it's their turn. Our format breaks that barrier — gently and regularly.

🤝 You're seen and heard. In a group of 7, the host notices your specific challenges — pronunciation, word order, missing vocabulary — and helps on the spot. It's as close to individual attention as a group setting can get.

🔄 You build a habit, not just a lesson. Our regulars come back because they feel the momentum. One session gives you a boost; consistent sessions build real fluency.

🌍 You find your people. Viva Lingua is more than a club — it's a community of curious, open-minded people who become friends. Many grab dinner together after meetups, go to exhibitions, or just chat in our Telegram group throughout the week.

📚 Free learning support. Our Telegram channel shares vocabulary notes, useful phrases, and recaps after sessions — so your learning continues between meetups.

🧪 Try it with minimal risk. Newcomers are welcome to book a trial session at a special price. No long-term commitment before you feel the vibe for yourself.

💬 FAQ

What levels are the meetups for?
English Speaking Club and KinoClub — for Pre-Intermediate and above. French Speaking Club — for B1–C1. One-on-one sessions are available for any level. If you're unsure, just reach out and we'll help you choose the right format.

How many people are in a group?
Maximum 7 people. This keeps the group small enough for everyone to speak and get personal attention from the host.

Can I try it first before committing?
Yes! Newcomers can book a trial session at a special price. Contact us via phone, email, or Telegram to arrange it — no long-term commitment needed.
