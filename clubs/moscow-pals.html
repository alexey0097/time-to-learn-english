Moscow Pals Club

The fastest growing English-speaking community in Russia. 🤝 Moscow Pals (Московские Друзья) is a community of more than 6,000 people, founded in 2022, where locals and foreigners connect in English.

Come in, make some new pals and share with others! 😃

Our Format

Format: a big community chat plus offline meetups and events. The community helps with support for foreigners, connecting local talent, jobs, meetups, events and much more.

🍻 Some of the offline meetups take place in a pub, where members chat in English at the bar and around the tables.

💬 One rule: be kind — bullying is not tolerated. 🚫
