Yoteach in touch

Yo, good people! 🙌 Yoteach in touch is a small, lively English speaking club at Surf Coffee® that combines hot discussions with board games. Jokes, laughter, new acquaintances — and lots of English practice.

The topics are all about people: why we're attracted to some people and not others, why humans are irrational, who stole our attention — phones, algorithms or ourselves? 🧠

Our Format

Format: a speaking club on one debatable topic, followed by board games in English. 🎲

  • Small group — maximum 10 people, so everyone gets to speak
  • A topic with questions — "Red Flags & Deal Breakers", "Psychology of Attraction", "Moral Dilemmas"
  • Board games — a tradition at every meeting
