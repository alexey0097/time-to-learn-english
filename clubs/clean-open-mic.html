Clean Open Mic

Bad week? Good. We don't fix problems — we laugh at them. 😂 Clean Open Mic is a stand-up comedy night in English from the team of English Stand-up in Moscow. Cozy vibes, friendly people and good laughs — the coziest comedy night in town. 🎤

Comedians — both locals and foreigners — try out their freshest jokes on a new topic every show, and the audience gets a rare chance to practice listening to live, natural English and laugh together with an international crowd.

Our Format

Format: a stand-up show hosted by Liko, with several comedians and a theme of the night — for example "Summer Holidays: how did we actually spend them?" or "Job".

  • 🎭 Clean humour — jokes without vulgarity
  • 🎲 Improv games with the audience
  • 🌍 An international crowd — expats and locals together

💬 Why go: it's a relaxed way to hear real spoken English with all its jokes, wordplay and cultural references — and you don't have to say a word unless you want to join the improv.

Special Formats

Foreigners & Locals: Stand-up in Russian

What do people joke about when two cultures meet? Everything! Language, traditions, mentality and love. From time to time the team hosts a show in Russian, where foreign and local comedians share the stage — the same clean humour, improv with the audience and a warm, friendly atmosphere.
