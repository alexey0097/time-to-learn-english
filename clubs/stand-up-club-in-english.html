Stand Up Club in English

Local talent and comics from all over the world. 🎤 Moscow English Comedy Events brings stand-up comedy in English to some of the city's best comedy stages. On any given night you might see comedians from Russia, the USA, Zambia, Colombia, Uruguay, Egypt, Turkey and beyond — all joking in English.

Some have been performing for years, others are just getting started. Come have some fun, make new friends and enjoy a laugh or two — it's also one of the most entertaining ways to train your ear for real, fast, natural English. 😄

Our Format

The team runs several regular shows:

  • 🎤 Big Sunday Show in English (Большое Воскресное Шоу) — the main weekly show with a host and a lineup of five or more comedians
  • 😈 Razgony in English — the super-popular format of Stand Up Club na Trubnoy, now in English: a handful of comedians sit down together, riff off each other's ideas and try to crack each other up. If you're lucky, you'll witness the birth of a new joke
  • 🌎 International Open Mic — an open mic in English on weekday evenings, so you're never quite sure who's going to show up

📸 From our shows

Comedians on stage, lineups after the show and the audience — in Moscow and on tour in other cities.
