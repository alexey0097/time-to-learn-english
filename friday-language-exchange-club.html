Friday Language Exchange Club

September 27, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Every Friday at 20:00 an international crowd takes over a bar somewhere in central Moscow and spends the evening swapping languages. 🌍 The meetups are run by The International Team (MO-EX) — that's the name you'll see on the weekly announcements — and they've been happening since October 2024.

There's no lesson and no host with a list of topics. You walk in, find the table with the loudest mix of accents and start talking — mostly in English, with Russian, Spanish and whatever else the table happens to speak mixed in. Expats, students, locals, regulars and people who showed up for the first time that evening.

Any level is fine. As the club puts it: if you can say "hello" and "thank you", you're good to go. 💬

Our Format

Format: a weekly language exchange in a bar — not a class. Nobody runs drills, nobody hands out a topic. People arrive at 20:00, push the tables together and talk for the rest of the evening.

  • 🌍 An international crowd — expats, students and locals from all over the world
  • 🗣️ Mostly English, with Russian and a few other languages in the mix
  • 🙂 Any level — "if you can say hello and thank you, you're good to go"
  • 🕗 20:00 sharp — the team asks everyone to be on time so the room fills from the first minute; evenings run to 23:00 or midnight
  • 🃏 Something to break the ice — usually a board game, sometimes karaoke, sometimes a dance floor

Newcomers are a normal part of every Friday — the announcements say it every week: whether you're a regular or joining for the very first time, you're welcome.

Two years of Fridays, in whichever bar had room for us that week — from October 2024 to this August.

Where we meet

The day and the time never change — Friday, 20:00 — but the bar changes most weeks. These are the spots that come round most often:

"Na Kover" is where everything started, and the club keeps coming back to it — a private hall of its own and a karaoke room. Everything else rotates, so check this week's announcement before you set off. It usually arrives a day or two before Friday and tells you which bar it is and how to find the group inside — past the bar and to the left, through the glowing curtain at the back, up to the private second floor.

Special Formats

Most Fridays are just talking. Some come with something extra:

🃏 Cards Against Humanity

The house game. Dark humour, jokes on the border and a very fast way to stop being strangers — the club calls it the ultimate ice-breaker, and it has had its own championship running across several Fridays.

🕵️ Mafia & Blood on the Clocktower

Social deduction nights at "Na Kover", run by one of the regulars. Classic Mafia or the bigger, meaner Blood on the Clocktower — hours of accusing each other in English. Seats are limited, so say in advance that you want one.

A few of those evenings — a hand of cards in the bar, the table mid-game, the Clocktower circle in our own room at "Na Kover".

🎤 Karaoke

"Na Kover" has a karaoke room, and the club has grown into the big one. Nobody makes you sing — but by the end of the night somebody always does.

🪩 Nights that turn into parties

Sometimes there's a DJ and a dance floor, sometimes a football match on the big screen, and occasionally something completely out of hand — a five-bar crawl across the city centre for Chinese New Year, for instance.

Nights that ended at the microphone, on the dance floor or out on the street on the way to the next place.

Price

There is no entry fee. The meetup itself is free — you pay only for whatever you order at the bar. The announcements put it simply: bring a smile and your positivity.

At some of the venues the club has a password — say "mo-ex" at the door and your welcome drink is on the house. It's been running at Bar "Zhazhda" and Bar "Davno"; the weekly announcement says when it's on.

Registration

Nothing to book and no list to get on — just turn up at 20:00. The one thing worth doing first is checking where the meetup is this week, because the bar moves. The community chat itself is a private group, so the Friday meetup is announced here:

  • Weekly announcement: the Friday meetup post on Telegram

Come alone or bring friends — everyone is welcome. See you on Friday! 🍻
