MoscowLEM

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Moscow's Language Exchange 🍻 — one of the biggest and longest-running language meetups in the city. Every Sunday evening, dozens of locals and foreigners pack a bar in the center to practice languages, socialize and have a drink together.

English is the most common language in the room, but you'll hear Spanish, French, Italian, German and many others. Just walk in, find someone who speaks the language you're learning — and start talking.

Our Format

Format: a free-flowing language exchange in a bar. No hosts, no topics, no schedule inside the evening — just a big international crowd, a friendly atmosphere and conversations that go wherever you want.

👥 Who comes: expats, travelers, students and Muscovites who love languages. Come alone or with friends — it's easy to join any group.

⏱ Drop in any time between 18:00 and 23:30 — people come and go all evening.

Our History

MoscowLEM has been running since 2013 — in December 2024 the community celebrated its 11th anniversary. For several years the Sunday meetups took place at Katie O'Shea's Irish Pub on Prospekt Mira, and in summer the group often gathered outdoors in Gorky Park. Since early 2025 the language exchange has been meeting at Green Dog.

Where we meet

Day Venue
Sunday, 18:00–23:30 Bar "Green Dog" (Zelyonaya Sobaka) — location

The language exchange takes the 1st floor and the small room on the 2nd floor. To sit outside or at the 2nd-floor tables, book directly with Green Dog.

Price

Entrance: free — you just order your own drinks at the bar.

Registration

No registration — just come on Sunday. Questions and chat with other members happen in the group.

Come for the languages, stay for the people — see you on Sunday! ✨
