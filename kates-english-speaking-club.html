Kate's English Speaking Club

September 27, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Kate's English Speaking Club ☀️ is a Saturday-evening board game night in English, hosted by Kate on the second floor of the StrEAT Food Mall at Avtozavodskaya. Everyone crowds around one long wooden table, the games come out of Kate's bag — Secret Hitler, Mafia, Imaginarium, Codenames — and the evening runs on banter for the next three hours.

It runs most Saturdays of the year, and the crowd is a proper mix: long-standing regulars, people who found the chat yesterday, Muscovites and people who moved here from abroad. Some weeks it's one table; on a good night, as Kate puts it, it's a full house.

A member summed it up to a newcomer in the chat better than we could: "They announce meetups here, usually Saturdays — we sign up, show up, and play board games." Or, in Kate's words: "Come for the English practice, stay for the laughter and friendly competition."

Our Format

Format: one big table, board games in English, and whatever conversation happens around them. No lesson, no host walking you through questions — you talk because the game makes you talk.

  • 🎲 The games: Secret Hitler, Mafia, Imaginarium, Codenames and Visual. Bring your own and they'll get played.
  • ♥️ Regulars and first-timers both — new faces turn up most weeks, and the table makes room.
  • 🗳 Sign up by voting "I'm in" in the poll under the weekly announcement in the chat.
  • 🕵️ Mafia has its own guru — Ihsan often takes charge of the night's games.
  • 📸 Photo of the Night — at the end of the evening the group votes on the best photo of the lot.

There is no level requirement. When someone asked whether A2–B1 was good enough, Kate answered: "Welcome to the club. You can only find out if you come."

Where we meet

Day Venue Address
Saturday, 19:30 StrEAT Food Mall, second floor 🎲 26 Leninskaya Sloboda St. — m. Avtozavodskaya
Summer Saturdays Kolomenskoe Park — picnic 🧺 m. Kolomenskaya, Exit 2

The start time drifts with the season — it has been 18:00, 19:00 and, since spring 2026, 19:30. Always check the announcement for the week. Once you're upstairs at StrEAT, the club sits to the right of the entrance.

Special Formats

🧺 Picnic in Kolomenskoe Park

When summer finally arrives, Saturday moves outdoors to the club's usual spot in Kolomenskoe Park. Bring a blanket, snacks and your great mood; Kate brings the board games, badminton rackets and a frisbee. The exact place is shared on the day by geolocation, photos and video — and if the weather turns, everyone falls back to StrEAT.

🥐 Turkish Breakfast

Every so often the club books out a long table at Bardak, near Kitay-Gorod, for a Turkish breakfast that runs all afternoon — warm bread, cheeses, olives, jams and endless tea, with Ihsan talking everyone through the food. Sign up in advance: the table has to be booked and the kitchen needs a headcount.

Price

Donations are appreciated — that's the whole of it. Every announcement says the same thing: no ticket, no fixed fee for the Saturday meetup, you put in what feels right. It's a food mall, so whatever you eat and drink you order yourself.

Special outings like the Turkish breakfast are a restaurant bill rather than a club fee, and get their own announcement.

Registration

No form, no deposit. Open the chat, find this week's announcement, vote "I'm in" in the poll underneath it, and just show up.
