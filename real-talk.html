Real Talk Speaking Club

April 26, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Guys, hello! Let's get to know each other! 💫

Real Talk is a speaking club that's been around for over 11 years. We host English conversation meetups in a speed-meeting format (sometimes it feels almost like speed dating 👀) in the center of Moscow — in cool locations close to the metro. 📍

We usually have 20–35 participants. Lots of interesting people and internationals. 🪐

Our Format

  • One-on-one conversations
  • Several 15–20 minute rounds
  • Partner rotation
  • Games in English during the second part

🕒 Duration: 2–2.5 hours

📎 Join our channel and check out the media to feel the vibe.

We're looking forward to seeing you at our next meeting! ✨

Rules

  • Speak English only — don’t worry about mistakes. We’re all here to practice, not to compete. 📖
  • Please order a drink or a snack to support the venue.
  • Don’t correct or “teach” your partner unless they ask for help — just enjoy the flow of the conversation. 🙂
  • Be respectful of other opinions and points of view. No insults, and don’t impose your religious, political, or other views on others.

1-on-1 conversations, switching partners every 15–20 minutes. In the second part — English board games.

Do I need to sign up?
Yes, better to sign up in advance. First time? You can just come without signing up. Foreign guests don’t need to register for any meetings.

How Does One Talk to People?

April 30, 2026 by Katerina Varenova

After 5 years of weekly participation in and hosting conversation clubs, I still don't know how to talk to people. 😀 But I'm trying.

I don't know any life hacks designed to win people over that wouldn't, under certain circumstances, turn into some kind of cringe.

  • You can ask questions, and the person will feel like they're being interrogated.
  • You'll want to cheer them up with a funny story, and they'll start yawning by the second sentence.
  • You'll spice things up by touching on a controversial topic, and now your conversation partner is fuming with indignation.
  • You avoid sharp corners, and you just blank out for 20 minutes, discussing nature and weather, never really digging deeper.

People are delicate creatures. So many circumstances affect us that it's baffling why someone who was happy to chat with you last time is now giving you the side-eye.

How to Be the Worst Person at the Speaking Club

April 30, 2026 by Katerina Varenova

Want your partners to widen their eyes at the sight of you and think, "Anyone but me! 🙏"
Want them to escape for coffee, to the bathroom, or to "get some fresh air because it's stuffy in here"? Then here are some tried-and-true anti-tips:

Solo performance

You've got 20 minutes to share EVERYTHING about yourself: work, hobbies, vacation plans. Your partner's trying to change the subject? But they haven't heard all the details yet! They decided to grab a coffee? No worries, you'll tell them the rest next time. 🎤

Correct every mistake

Your partner's making an effort? So what! It's your duty to help them improve. Correct every sentence, dictate the right way. Have them write it down. What else are we here for? 🤓

Stay silent. Always

Be mysterious and elusive. Answer every question with: "Yes," "No," "I don't know." If your partner goes quiet, that's a challenge — prove you can out-silence them. You're a pro. 😶

Don't hide your dissatisfaction

If you get paired with someone you don't exactly vibe with, jump up abruptly, wave your arms, and shout, "Not with them!" Let everyone know you value your time. 🤯

But seriously, you've probably met people like this.
