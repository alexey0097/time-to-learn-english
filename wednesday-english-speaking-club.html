Wednesday English Speaking Club

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Is it Wednesday today? Does that mean there is a meeting of the Speaking Club this evening? I think you know the answer to both of those questions... 😄 The Wednesday English Speaking Club is a simple, reliable tradition: every Wednesday evening, rain or shine, people gather at Checkpoint to talk in English.

The club is run by Peter, a native speaker with a great sense of humour — his weekly reminders in the channel are a small pleasure in themselves. The club moved to its own official Telegram channel in October 2025.

Our Format

Format: a relaxed conversation club — come, sit down and chat in English in a friendly company. No pressure, no textbooks, just live talk. In Peter's words, the only place that cheers him up on a grey Wednesday is Checkpoint at 19:00 — "it's the coffee and biscuits, obviously" 😅

Same day: every Wednesday. Same time: 19:00–20:30. Same place: Checkpoint. Price: 300 ₽.

📅 Wednesdays are never in doubt! The club meets every week, including summer.

Where we meet

Day Venue
Wednesday, 19:00 Checkpoint, Myasnitskaya St. 17 bldg. 2
First Saturday of the month, 14:00 Checkpoint, Myasnitskaya St. 17 bldg. 2

Special Formats

☀️ Saturday Speaking Club

After a one-off Saturday meeting in 2026 got a good response, Peter ran a poll among members, and the club brought back weekend meetings: a special Saturday edition takes place on the first Saturday of each month at 14:00. As the cool kids in the 1950s said, "be there, or be square"!

Beyond the Club

Sometimes the conversation continues outside Checkpoint. After the last meeting of 2025, the club went to the Kozlovna pub on Myasnitskaya, about 100 metres away, to celebrate Christmas and New Year — everyone was welcome, whether they had come to the club or not.

Peter also shares English-language events with the members: in February 2026 he invited everyone to a free concert by his Irish friend Rolf Hogan — Irish songs and 1960s R&B (plus one song in Irish Gaelic) at the Union Jack pub near Trubnaya metro.

Registration

No registration — just come to Checkpoint on Wednesday evening.

  • Club channel: @thewednesdayclub

Hope to see you there — rain or shine! ✨
