English Speaking Club at VDNH

September 27, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Every Wednesday evening a long wooden table at Surf Coffee x VDNX gets a "Reserved" card on it, and for the next three hours the conversation around it is in English. We have been doing this since February 2025 — same day, same table, no lessons and no textbooks.

There is a host and there is usually a theme, but nothing is compulsory. Dmitry drops a list of questions into the chat an hour before we start, always with the same note: "topics and questions aren't obligatory, use them if you want to". Most evenings the conversation wanders off on its own anyway.

A typical evening is somewhere between six and a dozen people. Some come to keep their English alive between exams, some are from Mexico, Iran, Turkey, Egypt or France and simply enjoy an evening in English. Everyone sits at one table, so there is one conversation, not five.

Our Format

Format: one big table, one shared conversation, from 19:00 until about 22:00. No pairs, no breakout groups, no one grading you.

  • ☕ One table, everyone together — you join the conversation that's already running
  • 📋 An optional theme every week — the question list is posted in the chat before we start
  • 🌍 Different accents at the table — regulars come from Mexico, Iran, Turkey, Egypt, France, Argentina and Belgium
  • 🎯 B1 and up feels comfortable — "ideally B1, but we're not planning to throw anyone out", as the host puts it
  • 📸 A group photo at the end — a club tradition, and the chat scolds the host when he forgets it
  • 🇬🇧 Slang quizzes between meetings — "a doddle", "gutted", "ta", "keep your hair on" and the rest of the British bits

💬 Topics we've talked through: books, sleep, coffee, films, music, unrequited love, anxiety, online dating, nightlife, white lies and honesty, medicine, aliens, France, body image — plus plenty of evenings with no theme at all.

Where we meet

Day Venue Address
Wednesday, 19:00 Surf Coffee x VDNX ☕ 2nd Ostankinskaya St, 3 — m. VDNH

Look for the long table with the "Reserved" card — it's right in front of the entrance. People usually stay until around 10 pm. Once, over the New Year holidays, we moved to Surf Coffee Cave in the city centre (Novokuznetskaya St, 6), but VDNH is home.

Price

Free. It has been free since the very first meeting — whenever someone asks in the chat how much it costs, the answer is the same one word. Surf Coffee is a working coffee shop, so the only thing you might spend money on is your own cup.

Registration

There is nothing to register for. Join the chat, vote in the poll that goes up the day before so we know how many chairs to pull together, and just show up on Wednesday.
