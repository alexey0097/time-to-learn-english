Reading and Vocabulary Club

May 5, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Free online reading club with a CELTA-certified teacher. Read authentic texts, learn new words in context, and actually use them in conversation. No homework, no preparation — just show up and practice. Making mistakes is absolutely fine. 😌

💫 Price: Free in 2026

💫 Sign-Up: Message Elena directly: @helen_9119 or leave a comment in the channel

💫 Where: Online (Zoom, camera on)

💫 When: Sunday 20:00 (60 min with a short break)

Level: B1–B2 (Intermediate). Not sure about your level? Elena explains it simply: you can chat on everyday topics, understand most news and fiction, and actively express your opinion. If that sounds like you — come join!

Group size: 4 to 12 people. Registration is required so everyone feels comfortable. If your plans change, just let Elena know — no hard feelings.

What we read: Authentic, non-adapted materials — book excerpts, articles, short stories. Past topics include the psychology of color and light from Joyful by Ingrid Fetell Lee, fake news detection, sleep science from Matthew Walker's Why We Sleep, and even a special Women's Day session with Gloria Gaynor's lyrics. 🎶

About the teacher: Elena Larina has been teaching English since 2014 and earned her CELTA certificate from Cambridge in 2025. She also runs free charity lessons for motivated students and believes that motivation is the best payment for success. ❤️

📢 Club announcements and schedule: @reading_and_vocabulary_club
