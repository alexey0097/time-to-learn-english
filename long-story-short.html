Long Story Short

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Hello, my dear co-readers! 📖 Long Story Short is a reading club at the Nekrasovka library where members read one short story a month and meet to discuss how it resonates with each of them.

The host carefully selects stories by the best short fiction writers of the 20th and 21st centuries — and members are welcome to suggest their favourite authors too.

Our Format

Format: a monthly literary discussion. The story and a short introduction to the author are announced in the channel a few weeks ahead, so there's plenty of time to read.

📚 Recent authors: Graham Greene, Martin Amis, Truman Capote, William Trevor, W. S. Maugham, A. S. Byatt, J. D. Salinger, Alice Munro, Margaret Atwood.

💬 Between meetings the host shares impressions of novels in the companion channel Pale Blue Book — join the exchange of opinions there.

Where we meet

Day Venue Address
Once a month, usually Thursday, 19:00 Nekrasovka library, 5th floor Baumanskaya ul., 58/25, bld. 14 (m. Baumanskaya)

The club takes a summer break in July and August. The new season opened on September 10, 2026 with a story by Graham Greene.

Registration

Register for each meeting on the Nekrasovka website — the link is posted in the channel with every announcement.
