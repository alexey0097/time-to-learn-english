English Chatter Club

No experts. No scripts. Just humans being human. 👽 English Chatter is a speaking club for those who love deep questions, passionate debates and silly jokes. Every meetup is built around one intriguing theme — "Is the Matrix real?", "Do you want to live forever?", "Is psychology a scam?" or "The Art of Being Silly".

It's a small, warm community where it's safe to drop the mask, laugh at yourself and actually connect with people. Stay curious, stay badass! 😎

Our Format

Format: a themed discussion club with games. Each meetup follows a thoughtful plan:

⏱ Session structure:

  • A warm icebreaker to get to know each other
  • Deep and honest discussions in pairs and small groups
  • A debate — for example, Team PRODUCTIVITY vs Team CHILL
  • A game that guarantees genuine laughter — charades, pantomime or storytelling

💬 Topics: modern dilemmas, psychology, luck, work-life balance, technology and everything that makes you think (and argue politely).
