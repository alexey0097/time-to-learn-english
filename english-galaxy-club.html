English Galaxy Speaking Club

May 5, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Free Zoom speaking practice with a real teacher! No homework, no boring lectures — just guided conversation in a warm, welcoming atmosphere. New to speaking? You can just listen until you feel comfortable. 😌

💫 Price: Absolutely free

💫 Sign-Up: Join the group via this link and the Zoom details will be shared there

💫 Where: Online (Zoom)

💫 When:

  • Thursday 19:00 — for A1-A2 learners (slower pace, supportive vibe)
  • Sunday 14:00 — for A2+ learners (role-plays, free discussion)

💫 How it works: The teacher posts vocabulary and discussion questions right in the chat. You join Zoom, get paired up by level, and talk. The host guides the conversation so you never feel lost. Participants say it's a «перезагрузка» for their English brain. ❤️

💫 Extra practice: English-only chat for texting between sessions: @englishgalaxy_speaking

📢 Club announcements: @english_galaxy_club

📺 Learn by playlists: VKontakte | MAX
