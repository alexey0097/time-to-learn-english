English Lounge

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Good morning, kotiki! 🔆 English Lounge is a cozy speaking club near Mayakovskaya where you chat in English in mini-groups of 2–5 people, meet interesting people and discuss topics that are anything but boring — from "Supernatural Abilities" to "Should You Lie on Your Resume or Just Lie in Life?".

Any level is welcome — just bring a good mood. 🤍

Our Format

Format: a speaking club with a host and one topic per meeting. You talk in small groups, so everyone gets plenty of speaking time.

  • 🤍 Any level — you'll feel comfortable with whatever English you have
  • 🤍 Mini-groups of 2–5 people
  • 🤍 A new topic every week — the questions are shared in the channel as a Google Doc

💬 Recent topics: "Autumn: Cozy or Depressing?", "Are We Getting Dumber Because of Technology?", "Emotional AI: Real Support or Fake Empathy?", "Moving Abroad Will Fix Your Life or Not?"

Special Formats

🕵️ Mafia & Bunker in English

Think you can read people? Can you convince everyone you're a peaceful citizen when you're actually the Mafia? 👀 Board game afternoons are the perfect way to speak English in a lively, informal atmosphere, meet new people and test your poker face. Recommended level — B1 and above.
