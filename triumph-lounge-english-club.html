English Club at Triumph Lounge Bar

September 27, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

"We don't teach here. We talk." 🍷 That is how this club introduces itself, and it is a fair warning — no textbooks, no grammar drills, nobody marking your mistakes. What you get instead is a big table in a lounge bar by metro Aeroport, a host called Kira, and an evening in English.

Groups are small and there is a moderator keeping the conversation moving, usually alongside expats from various countries and native speakers. Everybody can speak up or just sit and listen — both are allowed. "No filters, no judgement," as the club puts it. In warm weather the whole thing moves out onto the terrace.

It is a bar, so the evening comes with what a bar has: coffee, or something stronger, or hookah. There is private parking for guests, but the pass has to be ordered before you arrive.

Our Format

Format: a themed speaking club with a host. One topic per meeting, small groups and free conversation — with game nights and screenings dropped in between the talking evenings.

  • 🍷 Nobody teaches — it is practice, not a lesson
  • 🍷 Small groups with a moderator, expats and native speakers in the room
  • 🍷 A topic every time — talk as much or as little as you like
  • 🍷 Bring some English — if you can already hold a conversation, mistakes and all, you will be fine here

They like trying new shapes for an evening: board games, film screenings, debates, speed-friending. 💬 Recent topics: "Social Intelligence", "Boomers & Millennials vs Gen Z", "Work-Life Balance", "Guilty Pleasures", "Traditions", "Living Your Best Life", "Best Stories of Your Life".

Sunday is the standing meeting. The Tuesday evening sits on top of it and is announced week by week, so check before you plan around it. Start times have shifted by half an hour now and then — 17:00 on a Sunday, 19:00 on a Tuesday — worth confirming when you book.

Special Formats

🃏 Game nights in English

Some evenings the talking happens over a game instead. The club's favourite is cooperative poker — no gambling and no bets: it borrows Texas Hold'em combinations, but the whole table has to work out together, in English, who is holding the strongest hand and who the weakest. Teamwork, sharing information, logic and negotiation, up to six players. They have also played Bunker on a big game night, and once ran Blood on the Clocktower — a game of deception with 25+ roles and a native speaker as storyteller — at a gastro bar on Novy Arbat rather than at the lounge.

🎬 Screening nights

Every so often a meeting turns into a screening. One evening was Love, Death & Robots: a single episode, ten minutes on the screen, then an hour of conversation about it. Series and films come round between the talking meetings — the format is announced in advance, so watch the channel.

Price

Entry is 1000 ₽, paid up front — the club works by pre-registration, and prepayment is part of booking your seat.

More recent announcements word it as "from 1000 ₽" and mention season passes for regulars, so ask the host what applies to the meeting you want.

Registration

Write first, then come — seats are booked and paid ahead, and the Sunday table does fill up. Ask about the parking pass in the same message if you are driving.
