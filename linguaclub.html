LinguaClub

English without fluff, cramming or boring rules. 🐚 LinguaClub is a small online speaking club for advanced learners (B2–C1) run by an English teacher. Once a month, a mini-group meets in Zoom to discuss a thought-provoking topic and bring advanced vocabulary into active use.

It's perfect if you want to keep your English sharp, prepare for an exam, work or study abroad — or simply enjoy heated discussions with like-minded people. 🔥

Our Format

Format: a monthly online club in a mini-group of 3–6 participants, about 75 minutes.

⏱ How it works:

  • 3 days before the meeting you get preparation materials: an article or a short video, a list of advanced vocabulary and a practice exercise
  • At the meeting you get acquainted and share points of view, discuss the topic in the whole group and in pairs
  • Interactive tasks help you activate the new vocabulary in your speech

💬 Topics: chosen by a vote in the chat every month — for example, "Is AI making us smarter or lazier?" or "Things school never taught us".

Club materials

Cards from the club channel: who the club is for, how a meeting goes, the format, an advanced vocabulary list for the topic "Is AI making us smarter or lazier?", slides from past meetings and participants' feedback.
