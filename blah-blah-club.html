Blah-Blah Club

May 8, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Blah-Blah Club is an English speaking club in Moscow and Saint Petersburg. It's a community of people looking to level up their English conversation skills. The main idea is live communication in a relaxed setting, breaking the language barrier, and having a great time over your favourite drink. ☕️✨

Our Format

Every meetup starts with introductions: everyone shares a little about themselves and their interests. Hosts ask questions or run mini-games so participants get to know each other better. Then comes the topic discussion in different formats: round table, pairs, groups, debates. 🎤

Before the meetup, participants receive a list of questions on the topic and useful vocabulary — it helps you prepare and feel more confident. 📝 We wrap up with group photos and share our impressions. 📸

  • ⏰ Duration: 1.5 hours. People often lose track of time and stay longer.
  • 👥 Group size: 4 to 12 people.
  • 📊 Level: topics are aimed at B1 and above, but you can come with any level — just listen and gradually join the conversation.

💬 FAQ

🗣️ How does a meetup go?
A meetup starts with introductions: everyone shares a little about themselves and their interests. Hosts ask questions or run mini-games so participants get to know each other better. Then the topic discussion kicks off in different formats: round table, pairs, groups, debates. We wrap up with group photos and share our impressions. 📸

⏰ How long does a meetup last?
We set aside 1.5 hours, but people often lose track of time and may stay longer.

📊 What level do I need?
Topics are usually aimed at B1 and above. But you can come with any level — listen in and join the conversation when you feel ready. The friendly atmosphere helps break the language barrier faster. 🤝

📝 How do I prepare?
Before the meetup, participants receive a list of questions on the topic and vocabulary — it helps you prepare and feel more confident.

📋 How do I sign up?
To sign up, you need to pay for your spot on the website, after which you'll be redirected to a chat with our manager Angelina — just let her know which meetup you'd like to join.

👯 Can I bring a friend?
Of course! New participants are always welcome. Bonus — you both get a discount. 🎉

👥 How many people come to a meetup?
From 4 to 12 people. Spots are limited, so it's better to book in advance. You can cancel or reschedule no later than 24 hours before the meetup starts.

📍 Where do we meet?
Meetups take place in Surf Coffee® and other cafés in Moscow and Saint Petersburg. Club members get a 10% discount on drinks — just mention it at the counter. ☕️
