Say WHAT?!

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

Say WHAT?! | Скажи ЧТО?! 🗣 A cozy English speaking club in the centre of Moscow. It is not a lesson but a warm evening among interesting people: only live conversation — discussions, games and stories, with no textbooks and no marks. The idea is simple: forget about the "language barrier" and feel English becoming a part of you.

The club started in November 2025 with two sold-out launch sessions at Rebellion Cafe (The Pink Hub), and its Sunday meetings have been filling up ever since.

Our Format

⏱ A typical Sunday session (1.5 hours):

  • welcome and warm-up — time to order a drink, get cozy and chit-chat
  • an interactive conversation game in small groups
  • a group discussion where everyone shares their thoughts and ideas
  • club news and wrap-up

👥 Small group: no more than 8 participants, so everyone gets a voice and attention. The conversation is guided by a native speaker from the UK.

🗣️ Level: the first sessions were open to all levels — "it's about confidence, not perfection". Newer announcements describe the Sunday club as ideal for intermediate speakers and above who want to practise fluency and talk with internationals and locals.

🏆 Sometimes there is a small giveaway at the end of the club, and the winner gets a prize.

Where we meet

When Venue Address
Sunday, 18:30–20:00 (the first sessions in autumn 2025 were at 17:00) Rebellion Cafe (The Pink Hub) × Gavrosh Myasnitskaya ul., 24/7 bld. 1, entrance from the courtyard (m. Chistye Prudy)

The schedule for the week is published in the Calendar tab of the club's Telegram chat. Occasionally a meeting moves to another venue, so check the announcement before you go.

Special Formats

🎬 After Work Movie Club

On Thursday evenings at 20:00 the club watches a film in the original language (with subtitles), followed by an optional discussion in English and a short Q&A. Members vote for the film in the chat — Lost in Translation, Her, Frankenstein (2025), The Big Lebowski and Drive have been on the screen. Admission is usually free, but seats are limited, so send a message to save your spot.

💘 Speed Dating in English

A fast-paced evening to meet new people: a compatibility survey before the event, themed 1-on-1 chats, a live reveal of your top match and optional drinks together afterwards. 12 spots (6 women and 6 men), 800 RUB.

🍽️ Dinner in English

Delicious food and real-life language practice at a Friday dinner — 300 RUB plus your order.

🎄 Parties

The club also throws parties for its community — for example, a Russian–international New Year's party in a vintage loft in the heart of Moscow.

Price

A Sunday session costs 800 RUB plus an order at the cafe (it was 500 RUB during the first months). You get a refund if you cancel at least 24 hours in advance.

🎁 Bring-a-friend bonus: if you bring someone who comes to the club for the first time, you get 50% off your fee.

Registration

Places in the small group go fast and sessions often sell out, so book in advance: send a private message to the organiser — the contact is in every announcement in the chat.
