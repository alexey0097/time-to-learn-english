English Speaking Club

September 23, 2026 by Alexey Leonov

How about meeting in a cozy café, chatting with nice people, getting positive vibes and boosting your English together? Sounds like a plan! 😋 English Speaking Club (ESC Moscow) is an active community that meets almost every day of the week in cafés across the city center.

Speed meetings, game nights, thematic discussions, walks in the park, pub quizzes, mafia and movie nights — there's always something on, and new people are always welcome.

Our Format

The weekly schedule is built around three regular formats:

  • ⚡️ Speed Meeting — conversations with different people over coffee, a perfect way to meet new faces
  • 🎲 Talks and Games — board games in English. You don't need to know the rules: they're simple and explained in a minute
  • 💡 Thematic Meeting — a discussion around one topic, like "The best way to predict the future is to create it"

👥 Level: B1 and above for most meetings; thematic meetings are open from A2.

Special Formats

On weekends the club often runs something special — a great way to see the city and your fellow members from a different angle.

🚶 Walk & Talk

A stroll through one of Moscow's parks — for example, Vorobyovy Gory or Kolomenskoe — chatting in English in small groups. For everyone who enjoys walking in the pleasant company of like-minded people (and sometimes with a couple of four-legged guests 🐶).

🔥 Pub Quiz

An intellectual quiz in English with a proper pub atmosphere at Yonks bar: curated, mind-bending questions, thrilling competition and prizes for the winners. The September quiz is already the club's fifth, and all the previous ones were highly rated by participants.

🕵️ Mafia Game

The classic game of bluffing and deduction — entirely in English. Usually on Saturdays at Coffeeshop Company on Novoslobodskaya.

🍿 Movie Night

Tea, cookies and popcorn, a movie in English — for example, an episode of "Black Mirror" — and then a fun, relaxed discussion where you share your thoughts and pick up new vocabulary.
